How this summary is calculated

Each selected period is requested for the last complete calendar years, using the same ERA5 reanalysis model. Hourly samples are pooled. Temperature and speed ranges show the 10th–90th percentiles, not extreme limits or confidence intervals. Null samples are excluded, never treated as zero. Percentages describe this sample only.

ERA5 combines observations with a weather model on an approximately 25 km grid. Valleys, ridges and local gusts can differ substantially. A short sample is not a standard 30-year climate normal. No transport or dispersion calculation is performed.
